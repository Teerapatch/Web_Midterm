Recipe: Pizza Dip

Ingredients

Pull-Apart Bread

  • 16.3 oz can Grands biscuit dough
  • ¼ cup melted butter
  • 2 tsp parmesan cheese
  • 1 tsp Italian seasoning

Pizza Dip

  • 8 oz cream cheese, softened
  • 1 cup ricotta cheese
  • 1 cup marinara sauce
  • 1 ½ cups mozzarella cheese
  • Pepperoni (optional)

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F.
  2. Cut each biscuit in half, then roll into a ball.
  3. Roll balls in seasoned butter, arrange around edge of skillet.
  4. Combine cream cheese, ricotta and 2 tsp Italian seasoning.
  5. Spread mixture in bottom of skillet in the middle of dough balls.
  6. Top with marinara, mozzarella and pepperonis.
  7. Bake for 25-30 minutes.
